Work Samples

Various projects I've done or was a part of.

Collision Project

An early assignment within my sound design class. The goal was to get familiar with physical modeling and Ableton's Collision instrument.

Bass: A deep, resonant bass sound created with Collision.
Lead: A sharp lead sound designed to cut through the mix.
Pads: Smooth background pads for atmospheric texture.
Pluck: Short plucked sound with sharp attack.

Analog Synth Project

This project demonstrates various synthesized textures and presets created using modular analog synths.

Lead: Bright analog lead tone.
Pad: Warm analog pad for background.
