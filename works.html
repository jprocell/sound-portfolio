Work Samples

Various projects I've done or was a part of.

Foley Project

Simulating nature soundscapes with a variety of FM and envelope techniques within Albeton's Operator synth.

Summer Evening: A hot summer evening is simulated with various crickets, cicadas, and frogs singing as bottle rockets are fired off.
Thunderstorm: A heavy thunderstorm is simulated with multiple sheets of rain, droplets, and roaring thunder.

Sampling Project

An assignment within my Synthesis II class. We were provided a 30 second sample of cows mooing and asked to create various instruments only using the sample and Ableton's Simpler instrument. A quick musical idea was composed with these instruments, the isolated tracks are provided as well.

Mix: The combination of the tracks below within a short melodic idea.
Bass: Derived from sine-like waveforms found within the cow's moo.
Pad: Created by manipulating the sampler to loop a miniscule section indefinitely, while paired with a pad envelope, chorus, and reverb.
Pluck: Created used a similar method to that of the pad, but with a percussive envelope.
Drums: A variety of drum set sounds created using the sample and various parameters within Simpler.
Aux Percussion: Additional percussion created that didn't fit within the musical idea created above.

Collision Project

An early assignment within my sound design class. The goal was to get familiar with physical modeling and Ableton's Collision instrument.

Bass: A deep, resonant bass sound.
Lead: Bright lead sound.
Pads: Organ-like atmospheric pads.
Pluck: Short percussive pluck sound.
